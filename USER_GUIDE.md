# Scope User Guide

## What is Scope?

Scope is an AI-powered planning app that breaks down any goal, idea, or project into a structured, actionable plan. Whether you want to plan a trip, create a business strategy, develop a recipe, or learn a new topic, Scope transforms a simple idea into a detailed, hierarchical to-do list.

**Scope out. Dive Deep. Complete.**

- **Scope out**: Start with a high-level goal.
- **Dive Deep**: Let the AI generate a detailed, nested plan with actionable steps.
- **Complete**: Track progress, add notes, and bring your plan to life.

---

## Core Concepts: Folders & Scopes

- **Folders** are your top-level projects or categories (e.g., "Work Projects," "Vacation Plans"). All work is stored within folders. There is a default **Unassigned** folder for scopes that don't belong to a specific project yet.

- **Scopes** are the individual tasks, ideas, or steps within a folder. Scopes can be nested to create a hierarchy — for example, "Plan a dinner party" might contain sub-scopes for "Send invitations," "Buy groceries," and "Cook the meal."

## Generating a Plan

### 1. Choose a Persona

Select from the "I want to..." dropdown. The persona tells the AI how to approach your request:

- **Plan Something** — itineraries, roadmaps, schedules
- **Brainstorm Ideas** — creative concepts and mind maps
- **Build a Checklist or SOP** — step-by-step procedures
- **Analyze or Calculate** — market sizing, financial modeling
- **Learn or Explain a Topic** — structured lessons and breakdowns

### 2. State Your Goal

Type what you want to accomplish. Be as simple or detailed as you like:

- "A 3-course meal for 6 people"
- "A 5-day marketing plan for a new app launch"
- "A workout routine for 3 days a week"

You can optionally upload an image for additional context.

### 3. Review and Accept

The AI presents a proposed plan in a confirmation dialog. You can:

- Review the structure
- Provide follow-up feedback (e.g., "Make it vegetarian" or "Focus on a low budget")
- Add another image for context

Click **"Accept & Add Scopes"** to add the plan to your current folder.

## Views

Switch between views using the tabs at the top of the main content area.

### List View
Your primary hierarchical to-do list. Expand/collapse scopes, change statuses, add sub-scopes manually or via AI, rename, delete, and comment. Parent scope status updates automatically based on children.

### Mind Map View
A visual branching diagram of your project structure — great for getting an overview or presenting your plan.

### Kanban View
Scopes organized into **To Do**, **In Progress**, and **Done** columns.

### Execution View
A log of AI-generated deep-dive reports. Use the "Execute" action on any scope to generate a detailed case study.

### Comments View
A centralized feed of all comments and replies across scopes in the selected folder.

### Summary View
AI-generated progress summaries for a folder or scope. Keeps a history so you can track progress over time.
